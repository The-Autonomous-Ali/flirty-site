01 Who is responsible

Sameer Ali is the data controller for Flirty. Contact: flirty.app.support@gmail.com.

02 You must be 18 or older

Flirty is for adults. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and refuse accounts under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has an account, write to flirty.app.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

04 Payments

Premium subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card details. Google tells us only whether a purchase is valid and when it expires.

We will also disclose data where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to investigate a safety report.

06 Where it is stored, and how it is protected

Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your rows is restricted at the database level so that one account cannot read another's private data. Profile photos are stored in a bucket that is readable by other users of the app — treat a photo you upload as public.

07 How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it goes — see section 8. Aggregate figures that cannot identify you, for example how many accounts exist, may be retained. Backups are purged on our provider's ordinary rotation.

08 Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account inside the app: Profile → Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm twice, because it cannot be undone.

Deleting removes your profile, photos, swipes, matches, messages, subscription record, reports, blocks and ad-reward history. It is a deletion, not a deactivation — there is no hidden copy waiting for you to come back.

If you cannot access the app, request deletion by writing to us from the email address on the account, or use the web form. We complete the deletion within 30 days.

09 Your rights

You can see and change your profile data in the app at any time. You can withdraw location or notification permission in Android settings. You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, by writing to flirty.app.support@gmail.com.

10 Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app.
