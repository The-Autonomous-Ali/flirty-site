Delete your account

Deleting is permanent. It removes your account and everything attached to it — it is not a pause, and there is no hidden copy kept for you to come back to.

Two ways to do it

  1. From inside the app

    Immediate

    Open Flirty and go to Profile → Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm twice. Your data is removed as soon as you confirm the second time.

    This is the faster route. Use it if you still have the app installed.

  2. By email

    Within 30 days

    If you have already uninstalled the app, email us from the address on the account so we can identify it. We complete the deletion within 30 days of receiving the request.

    Email a deletion request

    Or write to flirty.app.support@gmail.com yourself.

What gets deleted

Everything below is removed. This is the complete list, not a summary.

  • Your profile
  • Your photos
  • Your swipes
  • Your matches
  • Your messages
  • Subscription record
  • Reports you filed
  • People you blocked
  • Ad-reward history
  • Your sign-in account

What we keep, and why

Counts that cannot identify you — such as how many accounts exist in total — stay in our records. Nothing in them points back to you.

Routine database backups are purged on their ordinary rotation rather than rewritten, so a copy may persist in a backup for a short period after deletion. It is never restored into the live app.

A message you sent someone else

Messages you sent are removed from our records along with the rest of your account. We cannot reach into another person's device to remove something they have already read or saved, in the same way an email you sent cannot be recalled.
