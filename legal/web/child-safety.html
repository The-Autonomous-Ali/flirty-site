Child Safety Standards

Flirty · com.flirty.app · Last updated 9 September 2026

Flirty is a dating app for adults. It is not intended for, and is not made available to, anyone under the age of 18. This page sets out our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), as required by Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Flirty is an adults-only service

  • Every account must confirm a date of birth showing the person is 18 or over before a profile can be created. The check is enforced on our servers, not only in the app.
  • The app is rated 18+ and its Google Play target audience is set to 18 and over.
  • We have asked Google Play to block users it has determined to be minors from finding, downloading, or purchasing within the app.
  • An account we believe belongs to a minor is removed.

Reporting a child safety concern

  • Any profile in Flirty can be reported from the app in two taps. The reasons offered include Underage, which exists specifically so a user can flag someone they believe is a child.
  • Reporting a profile also blocks it by default, so the reporter does not have to take a second action to stop contact.
  • Reports are written to a review queue that is read by a person. Reports naming a child safety concern are treated as the highest priority.
  • Anyone can also write to us directly at the address below, whether or not they use the app.

What we do about CSAE

  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation material (CSAM) is prohibited in every part of Flirty — profiles, photos, and private messages.
  • Content of this kind is removed and the account is terminated.
  • We report CSAM to the relevant national and regional authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and, in India, the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  • We preserve the relevant records when required so that authorities can act on them.
