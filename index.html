Nothing casual about this dating app

Fifteen people a day. No endless scroll, no bottomless feed — a small number of real profiles, and the time to actually read them.

A daily limit, on purpose

Everyone gets a set number of people each day. Swiping stops being a reflex when it runs out, which is the point.

Adults only

Every account confirms a date of birth before it can be created, and the rule is enforced on our servers, not just in the app.

Private by default

Your exact location is never shared. Other people see a distance, and the coordinates never leave our database.

Yours to delete

Account deletion is in the app and it deletes — your profile, photos, matches and messages. Not a deactivation.

Flirty is in testing and is not yet on Google Play. If you are here from the Google sign-in screen, that is the app asking only for your name and email address — nothing is ever posted to your Google account.

Signing in

Flirty uses Sign in with Google so there is no password to invent and no code to wait for. We receive your email address and the name on your Google account. Your email is only how you are signed in — it never appears on your profile, and you choose the name other people see.
